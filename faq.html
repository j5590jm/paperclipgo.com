Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where is my portfolio saved?
Strictly inside your own device's browser localStorage. If you clear your browser site data, your local portfolio resets—so we recommend clicking Export Tax CSV to keep a local backup file.

Does PaperclipGo work offline?
Yes. PaperclipGo v3.0 is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded, the calculator shell and your last cached price matrix work even without an internet connection.

How is PaperclipGo funded without selling user data?
We use cookieless static sponsor placements (AADS), an optional click-to-load non-custodial swap widget (ChangeNOW), and contextual links to self-custody hardware wallets and tax tools.
