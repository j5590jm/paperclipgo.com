Privacy Policy

1. Zero Server-Side Portfolio Storage: All cryptocurrency quantities, purchase prices, and scenario settings you enter are stored strictly in your own browser's localStorage (paperclip_holdings). They are never transmitted to PaperclipGo servers.

2. Anonymous Market Price Proxy: Live cryptocurrency prices are fetched through an anonymous caching proxy (api/market.php) that requests aggregate coin prices without exposing your individual portfolio balances.

3. Zero Behavioral Cookies: We do not run Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or cross-site tracking cookies. Our sponsor banner slots use cookieless static placements (AADS), and our optional ChangeNOW swap modal only loads if you explicitly click the Instant Swap button.
