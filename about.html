About PaperclipGo — Clarity, Privacy & Radical Transparency

PaperclipGo was built with a single foundational belief: you shouldn't have to surrender your financial privacy just to calculate what your crypto portfolio is worth.

Every portfolio position, purchase price, and scenario simulation on PaperclipGo runs 100% locally inside your browser's localStorage. We do not use user accounts, backend portfolio databases, or invasive behavioral tracking cookies.

All underlying cost-basis, ROI, DCA, and drawdown equations are openly published in our "Show the Math & Formulas" panel so you can audit every number yourself.
